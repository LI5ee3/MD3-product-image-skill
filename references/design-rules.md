# Design Rules and QA Checklist

## Output specification

- Canvas: 1200 × 1600 px (3:4 portrait), suitable for Ozon product main images.
- Format: raster image generated by GPT-image; no PSD, JSX, or web pipeline.

## Allowed content matrix

| Mode | Logo | Product name | Version text | Product PNG | MD3 geometry | Light/shadow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CREATE_MASTER | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| REPLACE_VARIANT | From master | From master | From master | New SKU | From master | Adjusted only |
| BUILD_SKU_SET | Master step only | From master | From master | Per SKU | From master | Adjusted only |

## Forbidden content (all modes)

- Any text beyond the product name and version text.
- Selling points, feature bullets, parameter copy, prices, ratings, badges,
  promo labels, QR codes, or watermarks.
- Extra icons, illustrations, characters, or explanatory graphics.
- Logos anywhere except the locked top-left position.

## Typography

- Product name: modern sans-serif, Bold 700, one line preferred; at most two
  lines when the name is long. Do not shrink below a readable size to force one
  line.
- Version text: Medium 500, placed directly below the product name and aligned
  with it.
- Do not change weights, positions, or sizes in variant mode.

## Layout and safe areas

- Logo: top-left safe area, fixed size and position after the master is locked.
- Product name: prominent, with a clear margin from the logo and from the top
  edge.
- Version text: immediately below the product name, following its horizontal
  position.
- Background geometry: must avoid the logo, product name, and version text
  areas.
- Product showcase area: centered or per master, large enough that the product
  is the clear main visual.

## Color and contrast rules

### Dark products (black, dark gray, navy, dark green)

- Use graphite or deep cool gray MD3 tonal themes.
- Keep the product the darkest element; use lighter surfaces or geometry behind
  it to create separation.

### Light products (silver, light gray, pastel, beige)

- Use warm gray, cool gray, or beige-gray MD3 tonal themes.
- Avoid backgrounds that are nearly the same lightness as the product; add
  tonal contrast and shadow.

### White products

- Never place on a near-white background.
- Use a clearly toned background (for example, cool light gray, warm gray, or
  a deeper tonal surface) plus visible shadow so the product reads as white.

### Text contrast

- Product name and version text must always be readable against the background
  geometry.
- In variant mode, text lightness/darkness may change only to preserve
  contrast; the text position, size, weight, and font style must stay locked.

## Variant lock rules

Allowed to change:

- Product PNG.
- Background main color.
- Background geometry colors.
- Shadow strength.
- Text lightness/darkness for contrast only.

Forbidden to change:

- Logo size and position.
- Product name size, position, weight, or font style.
- Version text size, position, weight, or font style.
- Product name-to-version spacing and alignment.
- Background geometry shape types, sizes, or positions.
- Product showcase area position or size.
- Overall composition or MD3 direction.

## State rules

- A master becomes locked only after the user confirms it ("母版确认" /
  "master confirmed" / approval of the master image).
- Once locked, never return to CREATE_MASTER for that product.
- A new product always starts with a new CREATE_MASTER, independent from other
  products' masters.

## QA checklist (run on every image)

1. Is the canvas 1200 × 1600 px?
2. Does the image contain only allowed content?
3. Is the product name correct, one line (or at most two), and Bold 700?
4. Is the version text correct, directly below the product name, and Medium 500?
5. Is the logo in the top-left safe area with no other logos?
6. Do background elements avoid text areas?
7. Is the product the main visual and clearly separated from the background?
8. Is the product's color rendered accurately?
9. Is there natural light and shadow giving the product dimensionality?
10. For variants: does the layout match the locked master exactly, with only
    allowed changes?
11. For BUILD_SKU_SET: was the master generated first and reused for every
    variant?
12. Does every variant look like the same master with a swapped product and
    theme, not like a new design?

Pass all 12 items before delivery. If any fail, regenerate with the
corresponding prompt; do not silently ship a non-compliant image.
